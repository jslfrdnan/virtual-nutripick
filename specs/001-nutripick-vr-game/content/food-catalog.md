### Integration Requirements

- FBX 7.4 binary, exported from Blender 4.x.
- 1 Unity unit = 1 meter; apply transforms; forward −Z; up Y.
- Pivot at the base center for food and drinks; pivot at the ground center for stands.
- URP/Lit materials; textures ≤1024 px.
- Food ≤5k triangles; stand ≤3k triangles.
- No animation, cameras, or lights in food exports.
- Food prefabs must contain `FoodItem`, a primitive collider, a Rigidbody, and a model child; `FoodData.prefab` must reference it.
- Stand prefabs must contain colliders, a sign anchor, and food anchors; they do not need a `FoodItem`.
- Name the FBX and prefab consistently with the IDs above.

### Placeholder Policy

- Use a primitive prefab (cube/cylinder/sphere) with the same `FoodData` and collider while an asset is missing.
- Placeholders must be replaced before final content sign-off; do not block M2/M3 on art delivery.
- If a placeholder remains at demo time, document it in the demo notes.
